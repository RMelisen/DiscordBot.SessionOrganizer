# The open camp: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the 5×5 plank hut that « syncs monte le camp » builds with the open camp in the
design. That camp has a fire in a trapdoor pit, two log-and-slab benches, a workstation on the
right, a bed in the back-right corner, four torch posts, and a patchwork floor of path and dirt that
stays after the camp is taken down.

**Architecture:** The layout is one table of strings in the body (`CampLayout`). It drives the site
check, the list of what to make, the placing, the taking down and the test. The bed is chosen in
this order: wool, then sheared wool, then a straw bed from what she carries, then no bed. The camp
reports whether it has a bed, and C# picks the line from that.

**Tech Stack:** Node and Mineflayer (`ProjectSYNCS/MinecraftBody/index.js`), C# (.NET 10) for the
lines, the local Paper 26.3 test server with ViaVersion and ViaBackwards (`tools/mc-pathtest`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-camp-design.md`. Read it first; this plan doesn't repeat
its reasons.

## Global Constraints

- **Version.** `config.yaml` is already bumped to `7.32.1` and not committed. HEAD is `7.32.0`. This
  batch adds user-visible behaviour, so make it a minor bump: change it to `7.33.0`, rename the
  CHANGELOG's `## 7.32.1 — 2026-10-22` entry to `## 7.33.0 — 2026-10-22`, and add the camp
  bullets to it. Don't bump a second time.
- **No commits.** The owner commits by hand. Commit only if the user asks in chat.
- **Language.** Lines and the `McHelp` text are in French, in her voice (read `docs/syncs-voice.md`
  before writing any). Code, comments, logs and the CHANGELOG are in English.
- **Comments.** Match `index.js`: each block gets a comment that says why, in the file's own style.
- **Response pools.** Use `{0}` for Papa or Tata. No stray braces (`string.Format` throws on them).
  Every new pool goes in the Checks pool list (`tests/ProjectSYNCS.Checks/Program.cs:2559`).
- **Nothing that isn't hers.** `CampOver` stays the only list of what she may build over. Flowers,
  saplings and mushrooms keep a site from being used.
- **The test server.** It is already running in the background (port 25599, RCON 25598, flat
  world). Before each camp run, do `node rcon.js "recipe take SYNCS *"` from `tools/mc-pathtest`.
- **The straw bed.** Her 26.1 client sees a straw bed as `yellow_bed`, both as an item and as a block
  (checked). Its book entry needs hay bales. minecraft-data doesn't know it, so it can only be
  crafted through the book.

## Review Focus

1. **A flower in the footprint.** She must pick another site and leave the flower standing.
   Covered in Task 1 (the poppy).
2. **Short grass over the floor.** It's cleared before the shovel is used, and the floor comes out
   right. Covered in Task 1 (the setup grows short grass).
3. **Interrupted between digging a dirt cell and refilling it.** On resume she fills the hole.
   Covered in Task 3 (`holes`).
4. **27 wheat and no hay.** She crafts the hay bales, then the straw bed. Covered in Task 4
   (`straw`).
5. **No wool, no shears, nothing for straw.** The camp is complete without a bed, and C# says the bed
   is missing. Covered in Task 4 (`nobed`).

### Task 2: The body builds the open camp

    offset of the block to click, e.g. `[1, 0, 0]`.
  - The task object `{kind: 'camp', player, window, site, placed, floor}`.
  - `camped {…, complete, bed, x: o.x + 4, y: o.y, z: o.z + 4}`, where `complete` means every
    layout block except the bed is placed, and `bed` means the bed is placed.

- [ ] **Step 1: Layout and site.**

  - The trapdoors (4,0,3), (3,0,4), (5,0,4) and (4,0,5), then the campfire (4,0,4).

    - About 60% of the floor cells are path.
    - (4,6), (4,7) and (4,8) are always path.
  - For each cell, in order of distance from her:
    - If the ground isn't in the grass and dirt family (grass, dirt, coarse dirt, podzol, mycelium,

    - Dig any short grass on it first.
    - `path`: equip the shovel and `bot.activateBlock(ground)`.
    - `dirt`: dig the ground block, then place `dirt` against the block under it. The dirt comes

    - Floor cells never go into `placed`.
