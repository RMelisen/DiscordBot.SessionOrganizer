# Combat Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** SYNCS's Minecraft body defends herself whatever she's doing, sizes each fight up (fight, fall back to her player, get out), and survives edges, ranged foes, crowds, fire and lava.

**Architecture:** The decisions that need no `bot` (fight sizing, weapon cooldowns, « surrounded », who she may hit) go in a new pure module `MinecraftBody/fight.js`, checked with `node --test`. Everything that moves her stays in `index.js`: one shared strike path for guard, patrol, defend and the new self-defence task (`kind: 'fight'`), plus hazard and emergency ticks. C# gets two pools and two event branches.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22, Mineflayer 4.39, mineflayer-pathfinder 2.4.5 (body); C# .NET 10 (service, checks); `tools/mc-pathtest` against a local Paper + ViaVersion/ViaBackwards.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-combat-design.md`. Read it and `docs/agents/minecraft.md` before any task.

## Global Constraints

- The body holds no words and no policy about speaking: no French in JS; every line comes from a C# `Mc*` pool through `ResponsePicker.Pick(pool, LineChoices)`.
- **She never places or breaks a block to survive.** No torches, pillars, hole-blocking, water buckets, digging.
- Everything in the physics tick is wrapped in `safely` and stays cheap (state ids, not `blockAt` per step in Movements).
- Goals change only on a mode change (`setMode`); a goal set every tick is never walked.
- Never hit family, a player, a creeper by hand, a tamed animal, an iron golem, bees, goats, llamas, polar bears, wolves.
- In sight only (`inSight`), never under water (`headUnder`), never through a player (`playerInLine`).
- `RetreatHealth` stays 6 (3 hearts): at or under it, always fall back.
- New pools follow `docs/syncs-voice.md`, plain game chat under 256 chars, are added to the checks' in-game pool list.
- Version: minor bump `7.38.0` → `7.39.0` in `ProjectSYNCS/config.yaml`, one `CHANGELOG.md` entry (English, no member names).
- Body integration tests need the local server (`tools/mc-pathtest/README.md`). If it isn't running, say so in the task report; never claim a scene passed without running it.

## Review Focus

1. **A monster hits her during an errand that can't restart** (waiting at a furnace): she fights, the smelt is dropped, the body sends `dropped` with `by: ''`, and C# says a danger line, not « {1} m'a attaquée ». Test: Task 3 `furnace` scene.
2. **A skeleton standing off on a hill while she farms** must not pull her off the field (twitching). Test: Task 3 `farmskeleton` scene.
3. **A player right beside the monster she's hitting** (Papa backed against a zombie): no swing lands on him. Test: Task 2 `beside` scene.
4. **Falling back with nobody around and a zombie that keeps up**: after the time limit she turns and kills it rather than run for ever. Test: Task 4 `alone` scene.
5. **A fight next to a ravine with lava at the bottom**: she never falls in, whatever knocks her. Test: Task 2 `ravine` scene.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (CommonJS exports of `fight.js`):
  - `GetOutKinds: Set<string>` = `warden`, `creaking`.
  - `NeutralFoes: Set<string>` = `enderman`, `piglin`, `zombified_piglin` (foes only once they've hurt her).
  - `RangedKinds: Set<string>` = `skeleton`, `stray`, `bogged`, `pillager`, `blaze`, `ghast`, `witch`, `phantom`, `breeze`.
  - `foeWeight(name: string, distance: number): number`: kind weight × distance factor (1 up to 4 blocks, linear down to 0.4 at 12, 0 beyond 12); `Infinity` for `GetOutKinds` within 16.
  - `strength({ health: number, armour: number, weapon: string|null, helped: boolean }): number` = `2 × (health/20) × (1 + armour/20) × weaponFactor × (helped ? 1.5 : 1)`; weaponFactor by tier: none 1.0, wooden/golden 1.2, stone 1.5, iron 2, diamond 2.5, netherite 3 (swords, axes, trident = iron, mace = diamond).
  - `sizeUp({ foes: {name, distance}[], health, armour, weapon, helped, previous: 'fight'|'fallBack'|'getOut'|null }): 'fight'|'fallBack'|'getOut'`. Order: any weight `Infinity` → `getOut`; `health <= 6` → `fallBack`; score = Σ weights; from `fallBack`, back to `fight` only when `score <= 0.75 × strength`; otherwise `fight` when `score <= strength`.
  - `cooldownFor(item: string|null): number` (ms): `*_sword` 625; `wooden_axe`/`stone_axe` 1250, `iron_axe` 1111, `golden_axe`/`diamond_axe`/`netherite_axe` 1000; `trident` 909; `mace` 1667; anything else or null 250.
  - `answerTo(name: string, { hostile: boolean, tamed: boolean }): 'fight'|'getOut'`: tamed → `getOut`; `GetOutKinds` → `getOut`; hostile or `NeutralFoes` → `fight`; anything else (`iron_golem`, `bee`, `goat`, `llama`, `trader_llama`, `polar_bear`, `wolf`…) → `getOut`.
  - `surrounded(angles: number[]): boolean`: true when there are 3 or more and the smallest arc holding them all is over π/2.
  - `openSide(angles: number[]): number`: the middle of the largest gap between consecutive angles (radians, same frame as the input).

Kind weights (`foeWeight` before the distance factor): 0.5 silverfish, endermite; 1 zombie, husk, drowned, zombie_villager, skeleton, stray, bogged, slime, magma_cube, phantom; 1.2 spider; 1.5 cave_spider, pillager, vex, shulker, piglin, zombified_piglin; 2 witch, blaze, ghast, breeze, guardian; 2.5 enderman; 3 vindicator, evoker, hoglin, zoglin; 4 piglin_brute; 6 ravager, elder_guardian; any other 1.5. Starting values, tuned in later tasks only through these tables.
